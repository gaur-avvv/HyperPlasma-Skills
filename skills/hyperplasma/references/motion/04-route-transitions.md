# Page and Route Transitions

Best for: multi-page sites, SPAs, case-study navigation. Always honor `prefers-reduced-motion` (swap to simple fade).

- **31. Cross-Fade Ambient Dissolve** [FM/CSS] - fade out one view while lifting the next (blur variant for a bleed effect).
- **32. Wiping Visual Curtain** [GSAP/FM] - solid color sheet sweeps the screen to mask loading.
- **33. Cinematic Iris Zoom** [CSS/GSAP] - viewport closes to a circle, then opens on the new screen.
- **34. Elastic Slide Over** [FM] - old page pushes out, new page slides in on stiff spring.
- **35. Glitch Strobe Wipe** [CNV/CSS] - alternating blur and flash frames wipe the screen.
- **36. Page Flip** [CSS/FM] - interface rotates 90 degrees back on a 3D Y-axis.
- **37. Radial Mask Reveal** [CSS/GSAP] - circular clip expands from the click point to fill the screen.
- **38. Modular Multi-Panel Split** [GSAP/FM] - layout splits into halves moving apart vertically.
- **39. Dissolving Matrix** [CNV] - panels disintegrate into thousands of tiny blocks on exit.
- **40. Bounce-Back Canvas Drop** [FM] - new canvas drops in with a small compression rebound.
- **152. Double-Wedge Asymmetric Split** [GSAP] - two color sheets close from opposite edges and lock together.
- **154. Glitch Strobe Compression Reveal** [GSAP/CSS] - screen compresses to a 1px neon line, then bursts into the next view.
- **160. Horizontal Multi-Panel Gate** [GSAP/CSS] - page splits into four vertical slats that rotate 90 degrees like blinds.
