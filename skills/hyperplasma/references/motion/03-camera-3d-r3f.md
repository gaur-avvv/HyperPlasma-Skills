# 3D Camera Rigs and Spatial Effects [R3F]

Best for: product showcases, immersive portfolios, games, data art. Keep draw calls and polygon counts low; use `powerPreference: 'high-performance'`.

- **21. Orbital Target Pan** - camera orbits a focal asset on a fixed radius.
- **22. Dolly-Zoom (Vertigo)** - move camera forward while changing FOV to warp perspective.
- **23. Macro Depth-of-Field Pull** - shift focal distance to blur background and spotlight detail (rack focus on click).
- **24. Infinite Z-Axis Tunnel** - drift through repeating portals; recycle meshes behind the camera.
- **25. Mouse Orientation Tilt** - cursor nudges camera vectors for parallax.
- **26. Spline Path Cinematic Cruise** - camera rides a pre-built curve through a 3D scene.
- **27. Raycaster Click Snap** - click a mesh to snap camera to its face.
- **28. Dynamic Glitch Shake** - short random camera jitter (120-200ms) to fake signal failure.
- **29. First-Person Free Look** - drag maps to panoramic angles with clamped vectors.
- **30. Orthographic-Perspective Morph** - interpolate between isometric drafting and true perspective.
- **147. Spatial Inertial Orbit Rotate** - orbit keeps spin momentum after release.
- **286. Real-Time Reflection Map Update** - metal reflections update as camera height changes.
- **287. Exploded View Assembly Snap** - parts reassemble as the camera nears focus.
- **288. Ambient Dust Proximity Wave** - space dust bends around the camera path.
- **289. Bioluminescent Edge Glow** - inner mesh glows based on camera angle/proximity.
- **290. Raymarched Quicksilver Sweep** - molten texture ripples when camera speed passes a threshold.
- **291. Cinematic Slow-Motion Drift** - drop simulation speed during view changes for narrative weight.
- **293. Multi-Pass Blur Post-Processing** - blur depth-based background, keep target sharp.
- **294. Wireframe Floor Displacement** - floor grid sinks under objects to show mass.
- **295. Holographic Scanline Overlay** - horizontal projector lines sweep across models.
- **296. Frustum Culling Optimizer** - hide off-screen assets during fast pans to hold frame rate.
- **297. Target LookAt Lerp Engine** - smooth focus changes with `lerp(current, target, 0.08-0.1)`.
- **298. Thermal Imaging Shader** - color by distance, deep blue to orange.
- **299. Chiseled Edge Normal Map Light** - light catches mesh edges sharply as the view changes.
- **300. Infinite Cosmic Nebula Travel** - drift through star fields; speed follows scroll.
- **425. Raycast Fracture Impact** - ripple/displacement at the exact polygon the cursor touches.
