# Scroll Storytelling and Timelines [GSAP]

Best for: landing pages, agency and portfolio sites, product walkthroughs. Clean up ScrollTrigger instances on route change.

- **11. ScrollTrigger Pin-and-Scrub** [GSAP] - freeze a full-bleed visual while scroll morphs the content around it.
- **12. Horizontal Rail Scroll** [GSAP] - vertical wheel input drives lateral timeline movement (add snapping for a rail feel).
- **13. Text Line Split Reveal** [GSAP] - split text by char/word and rotate it up out of an overflow mask.
- **14. SVG Path Draw** [GSAP] - trace SVG outlines/blueprints in lockstep with scroll.
- **15. Parallax Depth Drifting** [GSAP/CSS] - multi-layer art at different scroll speeds for depth.
- **16. Counter Tick** [GSAP] - numbers count up (0 to $10,000) with eased landing.
- **17. Image Reveal Displacement** [GSAP] - color mat slides off while the image shifts the other way.
- **18. Lottie Scrub** [GSAP] - map After Effects Lottie frames to scroll, with reverse playback.
- **19. Infinite Text Marquee** [GSAP/CSS] - seamless ticker that changes speed on interaction.
- **20. Grid Chaos Stagger** [GSAP] - offset pop-ins across a mosaic.
- **131. Scrub-Driven 3D Exploded View** [GSAP+R3F] - CAD model explodes into parts as you scroll.
- **132. Pinned Multi-Stage Split** [GSAP] - landscape stays pinned while three text blocks pass over it.
- **135. Focal Length Zoom Scrub** [GSAP] - fly through foreground text to reveal background layers.
- **137. Text Color Highlight Loop** [GSAP] - gray paragraph lights up word by word at screen center.
- **138. Sticky Video Timeline Scale** [GSAP] - centered video scales to full-bleed; scroll holds until done.
- **237. Page Scroll Progress Rail** [GSAP/CSS] - linear progress along the viewport edge with color shifts.
- **240. Text Highlight Underline Flow** [GSAP] - marker underline draws under key terms as they cross center.
- **241. Hover Proximity Custom Cursor** [GSAP] - custom cursor grows to mask media within target zones.
- **245. SVG Morphing Shape Backdrop** [GSAP] - backdrop morphs from blob to sharp polygon as pages advance.
- **246. Staggered Card Deck Reveal** [GSAP] - overlapping cards rise from the bottom and pin at sticky positions.
- **249. Alternating Multi-Row Rails** [GSAP] - rows of text move in opposite directions as you scroll.
- **250. Smooth Scroll Inertia (Lenis)** [GSAP] - intercept wheel data for smoothed, inertial page motion.
