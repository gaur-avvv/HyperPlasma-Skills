# Typography and Graphic-Led Styles

Type or print texture is the interface skeleton. Best for: editorial sites, agencies, portfolios with strong copy.

- **56. Typography-Centric** - fonts as the main graphic, extreme scale contrast over imagery.
- **57. Branded Editorial** - high-fashion magazine feel: generous margins, elegant serifs, soft palettes.
- **58. Kinetic Typography UI** - nav buttons stretch, dance, or distort on interaction.
- **59. Modular Typography** - words broken into geometric blocks that act as content containers.
- **60. Scrapbook / Collage** - polaroids, tape strips, handwritten notes, layered cut-outs.
- **61. Pixel Art (8/16-bit)** - blocky icons, monospaced labels, hard aliasing, arcade grids.
- **62. Glitch Art / Data-Moshing** - scanlines, scrambled text layers, flickering frames.
- **63. Vector Outline UI** - ultra-thin wireframe cells and unfilled transparent icons.
- **64. Halftone Graphic** - print-like ink dots for gradients, shadows, and backdrops.
- **166. Comic Book Panel** - bold black-bordered panels, action lines, dialogue callouts.
