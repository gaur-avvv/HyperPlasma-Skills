# Art Movements and Historical Styles

Fine-art philosophies translated to the web. Best for: portfolios, cultural/editorial sites, brands wanting heritage or intellectual weight.

- **46. Bauhaus** - "form follows function": bold primary colors, clean sans-serif, flat geometry.
- **47. Art Deco UI** - opulent geometric symmetry, gold foil trims, fine linear detail.
- **48. Art Nouveau** - sweeping organic linework, floral ornament, undulating borders.
- **49. Constructivism** - Soviet propaganda layouts: red/black, heavy geometric slabs, thick arrows, diagonals.
- **50. Psychedelic UI** - swirling marbling, melt-warped type, optical illusions, vibrating colors.
- **51. Gothic Digital** - dark ornate pointed arches, medieval calligraphy, brooding mood.
- **52. Baroque / Rococo** - ornate frames, theatrical deep shadows, golden scrolls.
- **53. Grotesque UI** - heavy industrial layouts, dense sans-serif, raw textures.
- **54. Expressionism UI** - distorted ratios, irregular sketches, emotional paint strokes.
- **55. Surrealism UI** - illogical menus, dreamlike art, surprising animations.
- **121. De Stijl** - Mondrian: black grid lines, asymmetric blocks, red/blue/yellow/white/black only.
- **122. Artisan Woodcut / Linocut** - hand-carved block-print look: heavy ink lines, stamp texture, paper grain.
- **123. Suprematism** - floating abstract shapes (squares, circles) over vast sterile white space.
- **124. Dadaist Anti-Design** - chaotic slashed type, upside-down imagery, mocks design logic.
- **125. Ukiyo-e Digital** - Japanese woodblock palette: seafoam blues, cherry-blossom pinks, calligraphic edges.
