# Color, Mood, and Genre Styles

Era, palette, and "-punk" worlds. Best for: brand-heavy sites, creative projects, landing pages with a strong personality.

- **28. Synthwave** - 1980s arcade: neon pink/purple, wireframe grids, glowing horizons.
- **29. Vaporwave** - Windows 95 prompts, glitch art, marble busts, low-fi purple-teal.
- **30. Cyberpunk** - high-contrast dystopia: electric neon, matrix panels, dense HUDs.
- **31. Y2K Aesthetic** - silver metallics, chunky geometric hardware, translucent plastic, millennium type.
- **32. Acid Graphic** - chaotic layouts, metallic liquid shapes, neon green, distorted fonts, anti-design.
- **33. Memphis Design** - loud 1980s pop: confetti patterns, squiggles, primary color blocks.
- **34. Pop Art UI** - halftone dots, heavy ink borders, saturated comic colors.
- **35. Dark Mode (OLED)** - true #000000 backgrounds for battery savings and edge-blending.
- **36. Monochromatic Minimalism** - one base color in tints and shades only.
- **37. Duotone Design** - two contrasting color overlays (early Spotify look).
- **38. Color Blocking** - solid contrasting tiles define structure without lines.
- **39. Retro-Futurism** - mid-century visions of the future, vintage dials, sepia hues.
- **40. Steampunk** - polished brass gears, copper pipes, Victorian type, analog instruments.
- **41. Solarpunk** - optimistic eco-design: solar panels, leafy motifs, warm earth tones.
- **42. Biomorphic UI** - smooth curves, fluid puddles, asymmetric living-organism shapes.
- **43. Kitsch Design** - gaudy, ironic, clashing nostalgic decoration and humorous icons.
- **44. Japandi** - Japanese minimalism plus Scandinavian function; muted woods, serene space.
- **45. Wabi-Sabi UI** - organic textures, rough asymmetry, earthy colors, beauty in imperfection.
- **120. Space-Age Pop** - 1960s space race: rounded TV-tube panels, turquoise/orange/cream starbursts.
- **126. Dieselpunk** - 1940s wartime industrial: rivets, greasy steel, military art-deco fonts.
- **127. Cyber-Noir** - neon cyber layouts with rain-slicked detective mood, venetian-blind shadows, monochrome.
- **128. Mythpunk UI** - folklore (stone ruins, runes) fused with neon cyberpunk data panels.
- **129. Clockwork Fantasy** - brass gears, key-wind mechanisms, roman numerals on white enamel tiles.
- **130. Raygun Gothic** - retro sci-fi comics: silver rockets, teardrop shapes, bubble domes.
- **281. Scandinavian Hygge** - extreme comfort: warm soft whites, natural light, highly functional layout.
