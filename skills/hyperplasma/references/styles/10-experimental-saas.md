# Experimental / Anti-Design, and Modern SaaS Defaults

## Experimental (art over usability; use sparingly, warn the user)
- **181. Liquid Typography** - type and elements sag, drip, or flow when hovered or dragged.
- **182. Brutalist Collage** - raw terminal fonts with jagged low-res scans and neon tickers.
- **183. Melted Interface** - elements sag and warp as if reacting to heat or gravity.
- **184. Anti-Gravity UI** - elements float free of columns, drifting with cursor physics.
- **185. Maximalist Typography** - giant overlapping type fills every pixel; buttons live inside letter counters.
- **186. Scanned Copy / Xerox Texture** - photocopy artifacts, edge burns, misaligned toner lines.
- **187. Anaglyph 3D** - offset red/cyan channels on text and images for retro 3D-glasses effect.
- **188. Thermal Camera Overlay** - heat-spectrum gradients (blue, magenta, orange) over images and UI.
- **189. Chiseled Inset UI** - text fields and lines look engraved into the background.
- **190. Kinetic Grid Matrix** - panels rescale in real time as the cursor moves across zones.
- **246. Anti-Grid Chaos** - elements dropped at random coordinates; overlaps and broken reading order.

## Modern SaaS defaults (safest, most professional)
- **65. Branded Corporate (SaaS Clean)** - standard modern app look: Inter, geometric cards, blue accent buttons.
- **191. Linear SaaS Clean** - dark grey mode, 1px borders, fine gradients, glowing feature highlights.
- **192. Enterprise Dashboard Minimal** - max data density, silver backdrops, compact rows, zero decoration.
- **193. Fluid Grid Bento** - see layout file.
- **194. Ambient Glow Framing** - minimal panels with soft color-matched glow shadows indicating status.
- **195. High-Contrast Accessible** - WCAG-first: solid black boxes, pure white text, stark yellow accents.
- **196. Monochrome Wireframe** - white canvas, black ink lines, placeholder boxes, no color.
- **197. SaaS Micro-Gradient** - near-imperceptible gradients across card fills for a premium curve.
- **198. Ghost-Panel UI** - no panel fills; thin 1px outer borders only.
- **199. Neomorphic Flat Blend** - flat cards with tiny dual-shadow borders for a slightly raised look.
