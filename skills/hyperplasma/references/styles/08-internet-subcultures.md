# Internet Aesthetics and Micro-Niche Movements

Born on image boards and social platforms. Best for: lifestyle brands, creator pages, communities, fashion, music. Risk: trend-dependent; may age fast.

- **76. Cottagecore** - rustic countryside: linen tones, botanical illustrations, soft vintage serifs.
- **77. Cybercore** - Y2K hacker culture: green matrices, wireframe meshes, low-res graphics.
- **78. Dark Academia** - mahogany, old leather books, fountain-pen details, gothic text.
- **79. Light Academia** - pristine library: warm ivory, cream borders, calligraphic icons, classical art.
- **80. Kawaii UI** - pastel base, round bubbly icons, smiling mascots, gentle feedback.
- **81. Coquette** - romantic light pink, lace trim lines, bows, delicate serifs.
- **82. Gorpcore UI** - outdoor-gear look: terrain map lines, carabiner icons, toggles, neon safety accents.
- **83. Liminal Space UI** - intentionally empty, eerie flash-lit corridors, dreamlike desolation.
- **84. Whimsigothic** - celestial moons and stars with moody velvet deep purples.
- **85. Weirdcore UI** - chaotic early-web amateur look, low-res camera artifacts, puzzling alerts.
- **86. Dreamcore UI** - pastel surrealism: floating clouds, glowing horizons, hazy windows.
- **88. Kindercore** - chunky red/yellow/blue blocks like early-childhood toys.
- **89. Seapunk** - 90s ocean: dolphins, neon turquoise tiles, floating grids, low-fi 3D.
- **90. Aesthetic Goth** - dark monochrome, white cross borders, typewriter fonts, grainy noise.
- **119. Pixel-Goth** - 8-bit grid gaming with emo/gothic imagery, pixel bats, purple-to-black gradients.
- **131. Neo-Goth Digital** - razor sans-serif, thin silver frames, deep ink-purple washes.
- **132. Goblincore UI** - muddy greens, mushroom icons, cluttered chaotic grids.
- **133. Space Core** - astronomical photography, glowing nebulae, interactive star maps, stellar coordinates.
- **134. Normcore UI** - default system fonts (Arial/Helvetica), white sheets, plain black text; anti-style.
- **135. Barbiecore / Hyper-Pink** - saturated magenta, high-gloss plastic, heart-shaped components, bubbly type.
- **136. Gitchcore** - oversaturated color channels, overlapping data layers, compression artifacts.
- **137. Angelcore** - airy ethereal: renaissance paintings, soft clouds, halos, faint gold filigree.
- **138. Ocean UI (Thalassophile)** - deep-blue gradients, ambient light rays, floating particles, pressure mood.
- **139. Desert Modernism** - terracotta, sage, sand tones with minimalist type and sharp architecture.
- **140. Hauntology** - faded 1970s TV colors, analog tape hiss, ghostly lingering shadows.
