# Morphisms and Light/Material Effects

Styles that mimic lighting, physical materials, or optics. Best for: portfolios, dashboards, AI products, hero sections. Common risk: low text contrast and GPU cost.

- **1. Glassmorphism** - frosted-glass translucency with background blur, layered depth, thin light borders.
- **2. Neumorphism (Soft UI)** - flat meets skeuomorphic; extruded plastic-like elements via soft inner/outer shadows.
- **3. Claymorphism** - puffy 3D clay surfaces, inner shadows, very rounded corners; playful.
- **4. Liquid Glass** - glassmorphism plus fluid animation, iridescent shifts, dynamic reflections.
- **5. Aurora UI** - minimal controls over blurred, flowing Northern-Lights gradient backgrounds.
- **6. Glitchmorphism** - digital errors, chromatic aberration, misaligned glass panels.
- **7. Skeuomorphism** - exact real-world object replicas (leather, metal dials, wood).
- **8. Goldmorphism / Metalmorphism** - chrome, brushed aluminum, or polished gold frames and lines.
- **9. Woodmorphism** - organic wood grain, matte finish, warm tactile depth.
- **10. Paperism** - overlapping cards with crisp card-stock edges and physical drop shadows.
- **71. Generative / AI Morphism** - translucent glowing containers with soft gradient orbs, like AI assistants.
- **92. Frosted Bokeh UI** - glass panels over out-of-focus light circles that shift with the pointer.
- **101. Prismorphism** - glassmorphism with rainbow refraction along the panel edges.
- **102. Plasticmorphism** - glossy clear vacuum-formed plastic, specular highlights, distorted backdrop.
- **103. Ice UI (Cryomorphism)** - frosty cracked-ice textures, high-opacity white borders, crystal patterns.
- **104. Mirror UI (Specularism)** - ray-traced reflections; containers mirror what's behind them.
- **105. Gellmorphism** - jelly-like viscous components with squishy physics and internal glow.
- **106. Glowmorphism** - intense inner/outer neon edge glow, as if cards were neon tubes.
- **107. Velvetmorphism** - luxury dark mode mimicking light-absorbing velvet with soft edge highlights.
- **108. Smokemorphism (Nebula UI)** - swirling smoke or ink-in-water effects inside transparent frames.
- **109. Ceramicmorphism** - glossy white porcelain surfaces, delicate reflections, deep shadows.
- **110. Soapbubble UI** - ultra-thin transparent components with crawling iridescent oil-slick edges.
- **175. Sandblasted Glass** - rougher glassmorphism with tactile grain that diffuses colors softly.
- **201. Quartzmorphism** - crystal glass with sharp facets, inner crystalline lines, prism scatter.
- **202. Biolumorphism** - deep-sea glow: organic vessels, self-illuminating pulsing states.
- **203. Photonic UI** - energy-field look: glowing vector paths, lens flares, laser borders instead of card fills.
- **204. Magmamorphism** - dark cracked igneous panels revealing flowing lava glow on interaction.
- **205. Gelatin UI** - containers with spring physics that wobble and jiggle when dragged.
- **206. Gaseous UI** - no solid boxes; shifting mist or cloud particles anchor the text.
- **207. Amorphous Liquid** - borders that warp, pool, and fuse like quicksilver drops.
- **208. Mercury Glow** - mirror-finish silver elements that ripple like liquid metal on hover.
- **209. Vitromorphism** - ultra-clear non-frosted glass, strong specular lines, deep refraction offsets.
- **210. Aerosolmorphism** - fine color-spray mist that builds density only where actions occur.
