# Layout and Interaction Structure

How information is organized in space. Best for: any project; pick the layout first, skin second.

- **11. Bento Box Grid** - modular rounded compartments of varied sizes, like a bento lunch box.
- **12. Minimalism** - no decoration; negative space, clarity, core function.
- **13. Maximalism** - dense layered graphics, heavy type, overlapping assets.
- **14. Swiss International Style** - asymmetric grids, stark formatting, pure typographic hierarchy.
- **15. Flat Design (1.0)** - crisp 2D, no shadows, gradients, or depth cues.
- **16. Flat 2.0 (Semi-Flat)** - flat plus subtle shadows and depth cues for usability.
- **17. Material Design** - Google's system: digital paper sheets, standardized lighting and motion.
- **18. Asymmetrical Layouts** - deliberate misalignment for dynamic tension and guided eye paths.
- **19. Card-Based UI** - uniform modular cards (Pinterest/Twitter feeds); scales for infinite scroll.
- **20. Split-Screen Design** - two halves for parallel journeys or strong contrast.
- **21. Full-Bleed Visuals** - edge-to-edge video or imagery with minimal overlay menus.
- **22. Brutalism (Raw Digital)** - unpolished HTML, monospace, exposed grids, functional asymmetry.
- **23. Neo-Brutalism** - pastel backdrops, thick black strokes, hard offset block shadows, cartoonish stamps.
- **24. Deconstructivism UI** - fragmented components, overlapping fields, broken continuity; avant-garde.
- **25. Horizontal Scroll UI** - navigation moves sideways across timelines or spatial grids.
- **73. Z-Axis Layering / Parallax** - background cards drift at different scroll speeds for depth.
- **74. Micro-Interaction UI** - looks flat until touch/hover triggers smooth physics reactions.
- **75. Bento Grid 3D** - bento compartments with floating interactive 3D assets inside.
- **143. Infographic-Centric UI** - the whole interface behaves like a live chart; dividers are data axes.
- **144. Split-Pane Matrix** - 3-4 nested resizable panels, like code editors and trading platforms.
- **145. Radial / Circular Navigation** - orbital menus unfolding from a central touch point.
- **146. Infinite Canvas** - no fixed edges; pan and zoom across a vast plane (Figma, Miro).
- **147. Node-Based UI** - boxes connected by drag-and-drop wires to show pipelines and workflows.
- **148. Stacking Card Deck** - panels slide over each other like a deck, building Z-depth.
- **149. Command Palette UI** - keyboard-first; a central search bar summons sub-menus.
- **150. Dockable Workspace** - rip panels out of the grid and dock them into custom columns.
- **153. Golden Ratio Grid** - layout and image sizes based on 1.618 for organic balance.
- **193. Fluid Grid Bento** - bento cells that stretch and expand on click to reveal detail.
