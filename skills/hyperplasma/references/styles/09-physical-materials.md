# Physical Materials and Textures (Paper, Stone, Metal, Wood, Fabric)

Best for: brands that want tactile warmth or heritage. Risk: heavy texture images slow load; use CSS/SVG noise where possible.

## Metal, film, organic
- **91. Brushed Aluminium** - linear grain and light reflection of industrial device casings.
- **93. Grainy Film / Noise** - subtle static or film grain over everything for a tactile feel.
- **94. Watercolor UI** - fluid paint blooms and canvas texture on inputs and buttons.
- **95. Cardboard Minimal** - tan kraft-paper background, charcoal ink, geometric folds.
- **96. Monolithic Concrete** - brutalist concrete texture, chiseled indents, inset lettering.
- **97. Chalkboard UI** - charcoal board with hand-drawn chalk lines and sketch fonts.
- **98. Carbon Fiber Tech** - woven dark grid for lightweight high-performance engineering.
- **99. Iridescent Chrome** - liquid metal with oily rainbow reflections on hover.
- **100. Leather & Stitching** - luxury padded leather with detailed thread stitching.

## Paper and print
- **161. Newspaper Editorial** - broadsheet multi-column grids, heavy rules, stark serif titles.
- **162. Manila Folder Archive** - tan paper, labeled tab menus, filing-cabinet cut-outs.
- **163. Passport / Document UI** - watermarks, rubber-stamp borders, guilloche security lines.
- **164. Grid Notebook / Graph Paper** - pale blue/green engineering grid; structured, educational.
- **165. Blueprint Negative** - black grid lines on neon green or amber, like vintage industrial copies.
- **167. Branded Catalog** - premium product mailer: clean listings, large prices, simple cards.
- **168. Vintage Postcard** - faded sepia stock, handwritten highlights, airmail stripes, stamps.
- **169. Classified Ads Layout** - packed grid of tiny text blocks and dense dividers; raw marketplace feel.
- **170. Crumpled Paper** - creased paper displacement maps make flat vectors look crushed.
- **176. Parchment Archive** - aged yellowed paper, fiber imperfections, sepia ink.
